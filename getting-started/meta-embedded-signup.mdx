## What it does

Epitomos manages your WhatsApp Business Account through Meta's **Embedded Signup** flow — a guided popup that connects your number without leaving Epitomos. Completing it authorizes Epitomos to send and receive messages, and manage message templates, on your business's behalf.

You'll find the **Onboard** button for this in the banner at the top of most pages until you've connected a number, or at **Settings → Connect WhatsApp**.

## Which situation you're in

| Your number is… | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Brand new, never used with WhatsApp | Choose "A new or separate number" in the dialog. This is the default. |
| Already connected to another provider's Cloud API | Turn off two-step verification for that number in WhatsApp Manager first, then connect. Meta requires this and it can only be done in WhatsApp Manager — there's no API for it. |
| Currently used in the regular, personal WhatsApp app | Not directly supported. Move it to the WhatsApp Business app first, or free up the number by deleting its WhatsApp account (around 3 minutes), then connect it as a new number. |
| Currently used in the **WhatsApp Business app** (on someone's phone) | Not currently offered. Meta calls this "Coexistence" — keeping the number live in the WhatsApp Business app and in Epitomos at once — and it isn't available in Epitomos today. |

If you'd rather not go through the popup, there's also a manual connection form that accepts your WABA ID and phone number ID directly, for numbers already registered elsewhere.

## Things that can trip up the connection

- **Two-step verification must be off** on the number before you connect it, or the connection will fail. Turn it off in WhatsApp Manager and try again.
- Embedded Signup both verifies your number **and** registers it for the Cloud API automatically — you don't need a separate registration step.
- Numbers added through the manual form are assumed to already be registered elsewhere, so Epitomos doesn't attempt to register them again.
