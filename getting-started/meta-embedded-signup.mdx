## What it does

After signup and onboarding, you'll go through a guided **Meta WhatsApp Embedded Signup** flow to either:

* **Register** a new business mobile phone number with WhatsApp, or
* **Migrate** a business phone number you've already onboarded to WhatsApp

directly through Epitomos — you don't need to go through Meta Business Manager separately.

Completing this flow grants Epitomos authorization to manage your business's WhatsApp communication on your behalf.

## When to use it

The first step of the [guided tour](/getting-started/signup-and-onboarding#guided-tour) that follows onboarding highlights an **onboard** button in a banner at the top of the dashboard — that button launches this same flow. You can also come back to it later if you skip it during the tour.
