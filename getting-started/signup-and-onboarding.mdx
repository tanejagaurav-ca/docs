## Signing up

There are two ways to sign up and create an Epitomos account:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Google">
    Sign up using your Google account.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Work email">
    Enter your work email address. Epitomos sends a link to that email for you to set up your password for the first time.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The first person to sign up for a business creates its **organization** (tenant) and becomes its **owner**. Everyone else joins by invitation — see [Inviting team members and roles](/feature-reference/team-account-management/inviting-team-members-and-roles).

## Onboarding wizard

The first time you log in, you're presented with an onboarding wizard as a blocking step over the dashboard. Completing it is required before you can use the rest of Epitomos.

The wizard collects:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | |
| Industry | One of: Legal, Professional services, Health & wellness, Restaurants & food, Tours & experiences, Real estate, Home & local services, Education |
| Business phone | A general contact number — not the WhatsApp number itself |
| Website | |
| Address | Autocompleted |
| Business hours | Opening/closing time, and which days of the week you're open |
| Time zone | |

<Note>
  All of these are editable later — your business name, industry, and contact details live in Settings → Profile; hours and time zone there too.
</Note>

Connecting your actual WhatsApp Business number is a **separate step** from this wizard — see [Meta embedded signup](/getting-started/meta-embedded-signup).

## What happens when you finish

Saving the wizard triggers a few things automatically:

1. **A "Welcome auto-reply" workflow is created and switched on** for your account, so inbound WhatsApp messages don't go unanswered from day one. Epitomos also fires a one-off test run (no real message is sent) so your workflow run history isn't empty on day one.
2. **A preview opens** showing a sample animated WhatsApp conversation with your business name, so you can see roughly what a conversation looks like.
3. **A guided tour starts**, spotlighting the main parts of the app in order: the "Onboard your WhatsApp number" banner, Chats, Contacts, Templates, Workflows, Integrations, Alerts, Reports, Settings, the Get started checklist, and Support & feedback. You can dismiss it at any point and it won't start again automatically.

### Trying Epitomos before connecting your own number

Until you connect a WhatsApp number, you can still try sending and receiving messages using Epitomos's **shared sandbox number**. Settings and the onboarding wizard both offer a join link and QR code — texting `join <your code>` to the sandbox number makes your account reachable there, so you can test your welcome workflow (and anything else you build) before onboarding a real number.
