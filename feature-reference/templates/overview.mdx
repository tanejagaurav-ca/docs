## What it does

Templates are pre-written messages you submit to Meta for approval, so they can be sent even outside a customer's [24-hour reply window](/feature-reference/chats/24-hour-messaging-window) — the only kind of message WhatsApp allows in that situation.

A template moves through a simple lifecycle: **Draft** (authored here, editable) → **Pending** (submitted, awaiting Meta's decision) → **Approved** (sendable) or **Rejected** (editable, with Meta's reason shown).

## When to use it

Create a template for any message you'll need to send proactively or after a conversation has gone quiet for 24+ hours — appointment reminders, order updates, re-engagement messages, and anything else that can't wait for the customer to message first.

## How to configure it

From the Templates page: start a new template, give it a name, language, and category, write its body (placeholders like `{{1}}` are supported, with example values), save it as a draft, then submit it for Meta's approval. Once approved, it's available to send from Chats and to use as a fallback message in Workflows.

Creating and submitting templates is restricted to owners and admins. You can also start from a pre-built example in the [Library](/feature-reference/workflows/using-the-library) instead of writing one from scratch.
