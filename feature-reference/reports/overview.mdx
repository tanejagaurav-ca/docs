## What it does

Reports show conversation and workflow activity over a time window you choose (24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or a custom date range): conversation and contact counts, messages in/out, first response time, conversation duration, how often a human took over from automation, and a breakdown by workflow and by tag.

## When to use it

Use Reports to understand how your WhatsApp channel is performing over time — response speed, volume trends, and which workflows are doing the most (or least) work. Test and dry runs are excluded from all workflow numbers.

## How to configure it

Pick a time window from the controls at the top — every table and chart on the page reflects that same window. Switch between daily/weekly/by-tag/by-workflow views using the tabs. Export the current view as CSV, or print/save the whole report (or a single table) as a PDF.

<Note>
  Customer satisfaction ratings and per-workflow conversions aren't tracked today, so they don't appear in Reports — there's nothing in Epitomos yet that collects a rating or defines what counts as a conversion.
</Note>
