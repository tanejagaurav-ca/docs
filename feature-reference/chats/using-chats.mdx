## What it does

Chats is the shared inbox where every WhatsApp conversation for your connected number lands — one place for your whole team, instead of juggling phones. Messages appear live as they arrive, and you can reply directly from the thread.

Each conversation shows the contact, the message history, and status indicators (unread, muted, archived, open/closed — see [Tags, mute, archive, and conversation status](/feature-reference/chats/tags-mute-archive-status)).

## When to use it

Use Chats day-to-day to read and reply to customers. If an automated workflow is running for a contact, it keeps responding until it finishes or you [take over manually](/feature-reference/chats/human-takeover).

## How to configure it

There isn't configuration here beyond your own conversation organization (tags, mute, archive) — connecting your number happens in [Meta embedded signup](/getting-started/meta-embedded-signup), and automated replies are built in [Workflows](/feature-reference/workflows/builder-overview).
