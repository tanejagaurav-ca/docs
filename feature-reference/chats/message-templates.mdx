## What it does

Message templates are pre-written, Meta-approved messages. Unlike ordinary chat replies, a template can be sent to a customer even after their [24-hour reply window](/feature-reference/chats/24-hour-messaging-window) has closed — which is the main reason they exist.

## Template lifecycle

```
Draft ──submit──▶ Pending ──Meta decides──▶ Approved
  ▲                  │                         │
  │                  └──────────────▶ Rejected │
  └─────────── edit ◀───────────────────────────┘
```

- **Draft** — authored in Epitomos, not yet sent to Meta. Freely editable.
- **Pending** — submitted to Meta for review. Not editable while pending.
- **Approved** — sendable from Chats, and usable as a workflow's fallback message outside the 24-hour window.
- **Rejected** — editable; Meta's rejection reason is shown so you can fix and resubmit.

## When to use it

Use a template whenever you need to reach a customer outside their 24-hour window — Chats automatically offers your approved templates in that situation instead of a free-form message box. Templates are also used as a workflow's fallback message when a step tries to message someone outside their window.

## How to configure it

Create and manage templates from the **Templates** page (see [Templates overview](/feature-reference/templates/overview)). Authoring and submitting templates is restricted to owners and admins.
