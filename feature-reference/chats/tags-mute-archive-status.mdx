## What it does

Each conversation in Chats can be:

- **Tagged** — apply your own tags to a conversation for organization and filtering.
- **Muted** — new messages won't raise the Chats notification badge, but still appear normally in the thread.
- **Archived** — moves the conversation out of the main inbox view, without deleting it.
- **Marked read/unread** — manually, independent of whether there are new messages.
- **Opened/closed** — a conversation's status, separate from mute/archive, for marking whether it's considered resolved.

These also work in bulk: select multiple conversations from the list and archive, unarchive, close, reopen, mute, unmute, mark read, or mark unread all at once.

## When to use it

Use mute for conversations you don't need pinged for, archive once a conversation is done but you want to keep the history, and tags to group conversations however makes sense for your business (by topic, priority, outcome, etc.).

## How to configure it

Actions are available from the conversation list (bulk, via selection) and from inside an open thread (single conversation). Tags themselves are created and managed from Settings.
