## What it does

The dashboard is the landing page after you log in. It summarizes activity over a time window you choose (24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or a custom range), and includes:

- **Conversation totals** — how many conversations you had, how many are waiting on a reply, and how that compares to the previous equivalent window.
- **A conversation volume chart**, bucketed by hour or day depending on the window.
- **Busiest hours** — a heatmap of when conversations happen, in your business's own time zone.
- **Active workflow** — your currently-enabled message-triggered workflow, with its recent run counts.
- **Open alerts** — a short list of what needs your attention, with counts by priority.
- **Recent contacts**.
- An **"Onboard your WhatsApp number" banner**, shown until you've connected a real number.

## When to use it

Check the dashboard for a quick read on how your business is doing on WhatsApp, and to spot anything (a failed workflow run, an open alert, a stalled conversation) that needs a closer look.

## How to configure it

The time window (24h / 7d / 30d / custom) is the only thing you set on this page — pick it from the controls at the top. Everything else is read-only; drill into specifics from Chats, Workflows, or Alerts.
