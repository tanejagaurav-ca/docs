## What it does

Flows are built and published from their own section of Epitomos, distinct from the Workflows builder. A Flow is designed as a form (its screens and fields), then published so it can be sent to customers — either directly or from inside a workflow step.

## When to use it

Build a Flow whenever you need structured, multi-field input from a customer in one exchange.
