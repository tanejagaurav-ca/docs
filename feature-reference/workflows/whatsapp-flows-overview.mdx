## What it does

A WhatsApp Flow is a structured, in-chat **form** — a native WhatsApp UI for collecting multiple pieces of information at once (think a short questionnaire), rather than a back-and-forth chat exchange.

## When to use it

Use a Flow when you need to collect several fields in one go — booking details, an intake form, a structured order — instead of asking one question per message.

## How Flows differ from Workflows

A **Workflow** is the automation engine that reacts to messages and drives a conversation step by step. A **Flow** is a single structured form that a workflow (or a direct send) can present inside a chat. A workflow step can send a Flow and then use what the customer submitted to continue the conversation.
