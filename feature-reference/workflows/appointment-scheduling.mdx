## What it does

Appointment scheduling is a workflow recipe: once you connect a supported calendar provider, Epitomos can let customers book, cancel, and reschedule appointments entirely over WhatsApp, checking real availability against your calendar.

## When to use it

Use this if your business takes bookings — consultations, services, tours, viewings — and you want customers to self-serve over WhatsApp instead of calling or emailing.

## How to configure it

1. Connect a supported calendar provider — see [Supported calendar providers](/feature-reference/integrations/supported-calendar-providers).
2. Adopt the appointment scheduling recipe for that provider from the [Templates gallery](/feature-reference/workflows/templates-gallery); a short wizard builds the booking conversation for you.
3. Customize the resulting workflow like any other.

## Booking, cancellation, and rescheduling

The booking workflow checks your calendar for availability, confirms a slot with the customer, and creates the event on your calendar. Customers can also cancel or reschedule an existing booking through the same conversation flow.
