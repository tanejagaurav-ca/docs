## What it does

Alerts are notifications raised by different parts of Epitomos — a new conversation from a first-time contact, a workflow run that failed, or something you or a teammate flagged manually. Every alert behaves the same way regardless of source: it shows up in the Alerts feed, a sidebar badge count, and (if you've enabled it) a push notification to your browser.

Alerts have a lifecycle: unread → read → acknowledged, or dismissed at any point. A system-raised alert can also resolve itself once the underlying issue clears.

## When to use it

Check Alerts for anything that needs attention without you having to go looking for it — a workflow that broke, a customer waiting, an integration that needs reconnecting.

## How to configure it

Filter and search the Alerts feed by status, priority, source, or type. Notification preferences — which alert types notify you, and through which channel — are set in Settings → Notifications, including registering your browser for push notifications. Muting a type stops it from notifying you, but it still appears in the feed; it isn't hidden.
