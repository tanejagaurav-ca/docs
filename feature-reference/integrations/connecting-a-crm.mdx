## What it does

Connect a CRM so Epitomos can bring its contacts in, and (depending on the provider) push contacts out, log WhatsApp activity against CRM records, and let workflows read and write CRM data.

Supported CRM and support-desk providers: **HubSpot, Salesforce (Sales Cloud and Service Cloud), Zoho CRM, Google Contacts, Microsoft Outlook, Clio (legal), Mailchimp, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Constant Contact, and Intercom.**

## When to use it

Connect a CRM if you already manage customers there and want WhatsApp conversations and contacts to stay in sync with it, or if you want a workflow to look up or update CRM records mid-conversation (for example, checking a support ticket's status, or finding a customer's deal stage).

## How to configure it

Most providers connect with **OAuth** — click Connect, authorize in the provider's own login screen, and you're done. A few (Mailchimp, Zendesk, Freshdesk) are **API-key only** — paste the key (and, for Zendesk/Freshdesk, your subdomain) instead.

Connecting and managing a CRM is restricted to owners and admins; other team members can see whether it's connected but can't change it.
