## What it does

Calendar providers power [appointment scheduling](/feature-reference/workflows/appointment-scheduling) workflows — checking real availability and creating events when a customer books over WhatsApp.

## When to use it

Connect a calendar provider before adopting the appointment scheduling recipe; the recipe needs one connected to build its booking logic against.

## How to configure it

Supported providers: **Google Calendar, Google Appointment Schedules, Microsoft Outlook, Calendly,** and **Zoom Meetings**. Connect one from the Integrations page — Calendly uses OAuth; the others connect the same way as their CRM/contacts counterparts where applicable.
