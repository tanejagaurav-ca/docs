title: "Field mapping"
description: "Mapping Epitomos fields to your CRM's fields"
---

## What it does

Controls how a contact's fields line up between Epitomos and your connected CRM when syncing.

## When to use it

Review field mapping if contacts are importing with the wrong information landing in the wrong fields, or if your CRM uses custom field names.

## How to configure it

Each connection has its own default field mapping; this is set per-provider today rather than freely editable in the UI.
