title: "Contact tags"
description: "Tagging and segmenting contacts"
---

## What it does

Tags let you label and group contacts however makes sense for your business — by status, segment, source, or anything else.

## When to use it

Use tags to filter your contact list down to a specific group, or to quickly see what kind of customer you're talking to from inside a conversation.

## How to configure it

Create tags from Settings, then apply or remove them from a contact's profile or from the contact list in bulk.
