## What it does

Contacts is your list of customers and leads — anyone you've messaged on WhatsApp, imported, or synced from a CRM. A contact is created automatically the first time someone messages your business, or you can add one yourself.

Each business keeps at most one contact per WhatsApp number or phone number — if a contact already exists for a number, new information (from a message, an import, or a CRM sync) fills in gaps rather than creating a duplicate.

## When to use it

Use Contacts to look up a customer's conversation history, edit their details, or organize your customer list with [tags](/feature-reference/contacts/contact-tags).

## How to configure it

Add a contact manually, or edit an existing one, from the Contacts page. Custom fields and notes can be added per contact.
