## What it does

Bring existing contacts into Epitomos in bulk, either from a spreadsheet or from a connected CRM.

## When to use it

Use this when you're getting started and already have a customer list elsewhere, or periodically to pull in new contacts from your CRM.

## How to configure it

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="CSV import">
    Upload a CSV, map its columns to Epitomos fields, preview the results, and confirm. Rows that would create a duplicate (matching an existing contact's number) fill in missing fields rather than creating a second contact.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="CRM import">
    Pull contacts in from any connected CRM — see [Connecting a CRM](/feature-reference/integrations/connecting-a-crm). The import wizard offers "Sync with Integration" as a source alongside CSV once a CRM is connected.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
