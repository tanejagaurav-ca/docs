Epitomos is a customer communications platform powered by WhatsApp.

**Tell us the business process. We turn it into a working WhatsApp workflow.**

Custom business workflows, integrated with the systems your company already uses, delivered through WhatsApp.

Epitomos helps you automate and streamline conversations with your customers over WhatsApp. Simply onboard your WhatsApp number onto Epitomos, and we'll guide you through creating interactive workflows that transform how you engage with your customers.

From initiating conversations to guiding customers through personalized interactions, Epitomos helps you automate engagement at scale while creating experiences that feel natural and conversational.

With Epitomos, you can:

* Automate customer conversations over WhatsApp.
* Create interactive workflows that guide customers through engaging experiences.
* Personalize interactions based on customer needs and behavior.
* Scale customer engagement without increasing manual effort.
* Drive higher conversions by engaging customers at the right time, through the channel they already use.

Epitomos turns WhatsApp conversations into powerful, automated customer experiences — helping businesses engage more effectively, convert more customers, and build stronger relationships.

Epitomos goes beyond chats, shared inboxes, and basic workflow builders. We design and deploy operational workflows around the way a business actually works — connecting WhatsApp to CRM, scheduling, support, contact, and industry-specific systems so conversations can trigger real business processes from intake through completion.

Epitomos transforms WhatsApp from a messaging channel into an operational layer — connecting conversations, business systems, and custom workflows from first interaction through completion.
